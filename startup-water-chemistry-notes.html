New Pool Finish Startup: Continuous Fill, Brushing and Water Balance

Fresh cementitious pool plaster continues curing underwater for weeks. Poor startup care during the first month—not factory material defects—causes most early mottling, calcium scale streaks, and premature roughness.

Key startup checkpoints to confirm with your installer

  1. Fill continuously without stopping: Wrap the hose end in a clean cloth to prevent gouging fresh plaster and run continuously until water reaches mid-tile—pausing overnight can etch a visible water ring.
  2. Brush walls and steps daily: Fresh plaster sheds fine alkaline dust as it hydrates; brushing with a nylon brush moves dust to the filter instead of letting it harden into rough scale.
  3. Hold off on wheeled vacuums, heaters, and salt cells: Confirm the exact manufacturer wait period (commonly 2 to 4 weeks) before running wheeled cleaners that can track soft plaster or adding salt for chlorine generators.
  4. Handle balancing acids safely: Follow OSHA chemical-safety fundamentals—wear eye protection and chemical-resistant gloves, add acid to water carefully with pump circulation on, and never breathe fumes directly over open jugs.
