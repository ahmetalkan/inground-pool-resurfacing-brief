Pool Interior Finishes: Plaster, Quartz and Pebble Compared

Cement-based pool finishes share the same basic Portland cement binder, yet adding hard quartz sand or smooth natural pebbles changes texture underfoot and resistance to minor water-chemistry swings.

  • Traditional white marcite plaster: Classic smooth finish and lowest initial cost, yet softer and more sensitive to etching or mottling when pH drifts out of balance.
  • Quartz aggregate plaster: Replaces softer marble dust with crushed colored quartz for a denser, more stain- and chemical-resistant surface while staying relatively smooth to the touch.
  • Exposed pebble / polished aggregate: Embeds small smooth stones into pigmented cement for long service life and textured depth; mini-pebble or polished blends soften underfoot feel for children's feet.

Ask contractors to show wetted physical samples in outdoor sunlight—water depth shifts perceived finish color noticeably toward blue-green. Explore finish options on Pool Resurfacing Guide.
